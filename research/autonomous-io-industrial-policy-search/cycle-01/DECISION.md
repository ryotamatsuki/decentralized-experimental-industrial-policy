# Cycle 1 — Decision

## Verdict

**NO-GO — KILLED AT STAGE 2**

## Reason

The proposed public-procurement lotting mechanism is structurally contained by procurement bundling, task interdependence, PPP contract design, and future-competition models. Chu & Wang (2015) is especially damaging because it already studies a current procurement bundle chosen partly for its effect on future competition. “Complementor entry” and “post-deployment concentration” do not constitute a new primitive without an additional mechanism, and prohibited rescue primitives would be required.

## Pivot instruction

Pivot away from contract-scope/lotting. The next question must not be a parameterization of bundle size, number of lots, bidder caps, or interoperability.
