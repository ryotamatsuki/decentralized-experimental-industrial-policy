## Prior-art judgment

The candidate's proposed theorem would be a combination of already standard
results: credible demand commitments increase ex ante investment; contract
horizon and renegotiation affect supplier competition; incumbent investment
can affect later entry/lock-in. The combination is economically sensible but
not a distinct mechanism under the user's novelty standard.
