# Cycle 05 — Question

## Candidate

**Advance public purchase commitment, supplier capacity investment, and later
re-tendering.**

## One-sentence research question

Can a credible advance public purchase commitment for an unproven technology
induce initial supplier capacity investment while deterring later supplier
entry when the government re-tenders, so that the commitment itself changes
future market structure?

## Why this is a nearby question

This cycle changes the strategic margin from Cycle 04's information release to
dynamic policy commitment and irreversible investment. It does not use the
killed public-anchor learning/disclosure mechanism, data-sharing mechanism,
lotting/bundling mechanism, coalition-composition grant, or a local
decentralized-experimentation mechanism.

The policy motivation is generic innovation procurement. Triangle Ehime is not
needed for the question.
