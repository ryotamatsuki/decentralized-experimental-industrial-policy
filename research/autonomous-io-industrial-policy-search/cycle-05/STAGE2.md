## Prior-art classification

- **Exact prior art:** No searched paper was found with the exact label
  "Forward Commitment Procurement plus later re-tender and future supplier
  count." This is not enough for a GO.
- **Structural containment:** Yes. Replace "FCP" with a credible long-term
  procurement commitment, "capacity investment" with supplier effort or
  investment, and "later re-tender" with the second procurement stage. The
  claimed behavior and welfare trade-off are already in the dynamic
  procurement/underinvestment family.
- **Primitive overlap:** Very high. Every strategic primitive—buyer
  commitment, supplier investment, procurement competition, contract horizon,
  entry/lock-in—is established in the closest models.
- **Result overlap:** Very high. Commitment can raise investment, while
  competition/renegotiation can change supplier incentives and future
  allocation. The proposed result is a special-case or relabeling risk.
- **Application-only novelty:** The FCP institutional label and innovation
  procurement setting would be the main difference.

The detailed pre-screen is in REDUCTION_TESTS.md. Fatal reductions include:

- **R2:** later supplier entry is standard procurement entry;

an unusual non-contractible continuation rule, or a new market-structure
primitive. Those are prohibited rescues and are not documented universal FCP
features.
