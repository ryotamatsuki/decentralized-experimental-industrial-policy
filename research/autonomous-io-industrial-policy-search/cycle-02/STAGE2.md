# Cycle 2 — Stage 2: Literature Kill / Frontier Audit

## Search conclusion

**EXACT PRIOR ART / HIGH OVERLAP — NO-GO AT STAGE 2**

The proposed mechanism is already present in several forms. The newest papers are close enough that “publicly supported deployment” cannot carry novelty on its own.

## Main prior art

### 1. Tong & Manea (2026)

[Data Sharing and Competition in Learning-by-Deploying Industries: Insights from Robotics and Beyond](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.00168), arXiv:2607.00168.

The paper models irreversible capacity choices where capacity in use generates data and future productivity. It compares fragmented and pooled learning under downstream Cournot competition and shows that pooling can raise output while depressing prices, reducing private sharing incentives, and even making the value of sharing negative.

**Classification:** exact prior art for deployment-generated data, capacity investment, competition, and the welfare trade-off.

### 2. Bhargava, Dubus, Ronayne & Shekhar (2026)

[The Strategic Value of Data Sharing in Interdependent Markets](https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/10.1287/mnsc.2024.04938), *Management Science* 72(2), 1472–1488, DOI 10.1287/mnsc.2024.04938.

The paper shows that firms' data-sharing choices interact strategically with interdependent-market competition; sharing can soften or intensify competition and can be privately valuable for strategic reasons.

**Classification:** exact prior art for the strategic data-sharing/competition margin.

### 3. Fang & Kim (2026)

[Data Neutrality, Data Supply, and Market Competition](https://www.nber.org/papers/w35159), NBER Working Paper 35159, DOI 10.3386/w35159.

The paper studies equal access to platform data, downstream competition, and the platform's incentive to produce/refine data. It identifies the same access-versus-data-supply welfare tension proposed here.

**Classification:** exact prior art for a government access/neutrality condition.

### 4. Hu, Hu & Yang (2017)

[Open or Closed? Technology Sharing, Supplier Investment, and Competition](https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/10.1287/msom.2016.0598), *Manufacturing & Service Operations Management* 19(1), 132–149, DOI 10.1287/msom.2016.0598.

The paper studies open technology, supplier investment, and future competition, including the possibility that firms close technology to induce supplier investment.

**Classification:** structural containment for open/closed technology and investment incentives.

### 5. Hagiu & Wright (2023)

[Data-enabled learning, network effects, and competitive advantage](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/1756-2171.12453), *RAND Journal of Economics* 54(4), 638–667, DOI 10.1111/1756-2171.12453.

The paper connects data-enabled learning, network effects, and competitive advantage in a product-market environment.

**Classification:** primitive overlap; the proposed data stock and downstream competitive advantage are already a mature IO mechanism.

## Kill tests

- **Exact mechanism:** Tong & Manea directly studies deployment-generated data and downstream competition.
- **Policy translation:** Fang & Kim already studies an access/neutrality rule that balances competition against data supply.
- **Open/closed result:** Hu et al. already obtain the private-investment versus future-competition trade-off.
- **Public support as novelty:** replacing a platform's data rule with a public-program condition is application-only unless public support creates a new strategic state; no such state is available without prohibited additions.
- **C5/C7 boundary:** adding regions, portability across jurisdictions, or open membership would move toward the frozen backups and is not a permitted rescue.
- **C6 boundary:** treating the data as a public signal or test result would reintroduce the killed evidence-design branch.

## Stage 2 decision

**NO-GO.** The candidate is killed as exact/structural overlap. The run must pivot away from data-access/technology-openness as the strategic object.
