# Cycle 2 — Decision

## Verdict

**NO-GO — KILLED AT STAGE 2**

## Reason

The desired result—open access improves downstream competition but weakens deployment/data-production incentives—is now directly analyzed by Tong & Manea (2026), Fang & Kim (2026), and Bhargava et al. (2026), with older open-technology theory providing structural containment. Public support is an institutional wrapper, not a new strategic mechanism.

## Pivot instruction

Pivot away from data access, openness, portability, and deployment-generated learning. The next cycle changes the strategic margin to organizational form and vertical boundaries.
