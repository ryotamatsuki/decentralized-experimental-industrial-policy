# Cycle 3 — Stage 1: Source & Institutional Audit

## Primary institutional source

[Directive 2014/24/EU, Article 63 and related provisions](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX%3A32014L0024) states that an economic operator may rely on the capacities of other entities regardless of the legal nature of its links, subject to proof of resource availability and verification. The directive also provides for groups of economic operators and permits a contracting authority, for works/services or installation operations, to require critical tasks to be performed directly by the tenderer or by a group participant.

This is a primary legal source for the eligibility contrast.

## Primitive-by-primitive audit

| primitive | source-supported? | finding |
|---|---|---|
| Prime bidder may rely on another entity's capacity | yes | Article 63 directly supports it. |
| Groups of operators may participate | yes | The directive recognizes groups/temporary associations. |
| Direct performance of critical tasks may be required | yes | The directive expressly permits the condition in specified contract types. |
| Legal form is not generally required at bid stage | yes | Groups are not generally required to adopt a specific legal form to submit a tender. |
| Integration is an alternative to subcontracting | theory plus business organization | The rule does not say firms will integrate; that is the strategic response to model. |
| Public payment goes to prime/awarded group | ordinary procurement form | The rule is a contract-performance regime, not a general grant transfer. |
| Foreclosure of downstream complementors | no | A possible IO implication, not an institutional fact. |
| Innovation investment effect | no | Must be derived, not asserted from the directive. |

## Timing

Eligibility is specified before tendering. The prime chooses an organizational form before or during bidding; performance follows award. The rule is compatible with the model timing.

## Institutional limitation

The legal text supports both external capacity and direct-performance constraints. It does not establish that direct performance is used to pursue industrial policy or that integration is technologically efficient. Those are model hypotheses.

## Stage 1 decision

**GO TO STAGE 2.** The primitive is real and generalizable, with theory clearly separated from the legal fact. The decisive issue is whether the organizational and competition result survives the mature vertical/subcontracting literature.
