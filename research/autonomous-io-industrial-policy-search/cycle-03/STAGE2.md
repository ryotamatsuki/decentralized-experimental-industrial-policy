# Cycle 3 — Stage 2: Literature Kill / Frontier Audit

## Search conclusion

**HIGH OVERLAP / STRUCTURAL CONTAINMENT — NO-GO AT STAGE 2**

The proposed result is already represented by vertical integration, PPP task bundling, procurement entry/subcontracting, and foreclosure theory. The public eligibility rule changes the parameter or constraint; it does not create a new strategic interaction.

## Main prior art

### 1. Branzoli & Decarolis (2015)

[Entry and Subcontracting in Public Procurement Auctions](https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/10.1287/mnsc.2014.2071), *Management Science* 61(12), 2945–2962, DOI 10.1287/mnsc.2014.2071.

The paper directly studies how public-procurement auction format affects both entry and subcontracting.

**Classification:** exact primitive overlap for the entry/subcontracting margin.

### 2. Rosa (2024)

[Subcontracting requirements and the cost of government procurement](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/1756-2171.12456), *RAND Journal of Economics* 55(1), 3–32, DOI 10.1111/1756-2171.12456.

The paper models procurement with endogenous subcontracting and policy requirements, showing how those requirements affect information, bidding markups, participation, and procurement cost.

**Classification:** structural containment for a public rule changing subcontracting choices.

### 3. Albalate, Bel & Geddes (2017)

[How Much Vertical Integration? Contractual Choice and Public–Private Partnerships in the United States](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11151-016-9540-1), *Review of Industrial Organization* 51(1), 25–42, DOI 10.1007/s11151-016-9540-1.

The paper studies the choice between integrating operational and construction tasks versus narrower contractual scope in PPPs, with risk transfer, market size, sector, and financial variables.

**Classification:** exact public-contract organizational-boundary overlap.

### 4. Yan, Ren & Pan (2025)

[Government procurement and corporate vertical specialization](https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/finlet/v77y2025ics154461232500323x.html), *Finance Research Letters* 77, article 107059, DOI 10.1016/j.frl.2025.107059.

The paper reports a positive relationship between government procurement and corporate vertical specialization and identifies transaction-cost reduction as a channel.

**Classification:** current direct evidence against claiming that public procurement's effect on vertical boundaries is an unoccupied question.

### 5. Bisceglia, Padilla, Piccolo & Shekhar (2022)

[Vertical integration, innovation and foreclosure with competing ecosystems](https://research.tilburguniversity.edu/en/publications/vertical-integration-innovation-and-foreclosure-with-competing-ec/), *Information Economics and Policy* 60, article 100981, DOI 10.1016/j.infoecopol.2022.100981.

The paper analyzes an integrated closed ecosystem competing with an open ecosystem, including innovation and foreclosure.

**Classification:** structural containment for the proposed integration/foreclosure/innovation result.

### 6. Zhang & Tong (2021)

[How Vertical Integration Affects Firm Innovation: Quasi-Experimental Evidence](https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/10.1287/orsc.2020.1396), *Organization Science* 32(2), 455–479, DOI 10.1287/orsc.2020.1396.

The paper studies how vertical integration affects innovation, reinforcing that the innovation consequence of firm boundaries is a mature object.

**Classification:** result-overlap threat for innovation claims.

## Kill tests

- **Entry/subcontracting:** Branzoli–Decarolis and Rosa already model public procurement rules that alter subcontracting and entry.
- **Public organizational choice:** Albalate–Bel–Geddes directly study integrated versus non-integrated PPP tasks.
- **Foreclosure/innovation:** Bisceglia et al. already supply the relevant downstream competition and ecosystem mechanism.
- **Eligibility novelty:** Article 63 changes which organizational form is eligible; it does not create a new payoff interaction. A direct-performance condition is a constraint on make-or-buy.
- **Policy-induced market structure:** the claimed concentration effect follows from standard exclusion/entry logic once the condition is imposed.
- **Referee test:** a competent referee would ask why a qualification constraint is not just a procurement/subcontracting or vertical-integration model.

## Stage 2 decision

**NO-GO.** This branch is killed at Stage 2. Adding joint liability, innovation quality, local suppliers, or an ecosystem label would be prohibited rescue or application-only novelty.
