# Cycle 4 — Question

## Candidate

**Public-anchor learning and the timing of entry**

Does the timing of a public pilot's verifiable performance signal change whether a potential entrant waits or preempts an incumbent when the selected supplier also accumulates a supplier-specific learning stock, and can the release date therefore change future concentration?

## Scope boundary

This is not a re-run of public reference-market formation, open data, C5/C7, or Q6. The frozen object is the timing of a public signal relative to entry and supplier-specific learning. No deployment-data sharing or multi-region diffusion is admitted.
