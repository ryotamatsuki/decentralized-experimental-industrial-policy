## Selection

Cycle 05 will test Question 1: whether a forward public purchase commitment
creates supplier capacity investment but can also deter later entry when the
government re-tenders. This changes the strategic margin from information
release to dynamic commitment and investment. It is not the killed pilot
learning/disclosure question and is screened afresh at Stage 0.
