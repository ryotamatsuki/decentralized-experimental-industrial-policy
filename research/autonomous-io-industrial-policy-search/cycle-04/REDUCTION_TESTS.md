## Freeze for Stage 4

Stage 4 may use only:

- one government/public buyer;
- one selected incumbent supplier;
- one potential entrant;
- one high/low verifiable pilot signal;
- one supplier-specific learning advantage;
- early versus late signal observation;
- two private periods and a later competitive market;
- a government welfare function including entry/consumer surplus and pilot cost.

Stage 4 may not add data sharing, multiple regions, multiple suppliers, a new subsidy, platform effects, standards, vertical integration, or a second signal.
