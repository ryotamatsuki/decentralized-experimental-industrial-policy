## Current closest-paper ranking

1. **Callander & Harstad (2015)** — closest on jurisdictions, experimentation, cross-region learning/free riding, centralization.
2. **Acemoglu, Bimpikis & Ozdaglar (2011)** — closest on private experiments, timing, copying and appropriability.
3. **Chiappinelli, Giuffrida & Spagnolo (2025)** plus the PPI theory it surveys — closest on public buyer/sponsor, private supplier, deployment risk, commercialization and supplier rents.
4. **Hausmann & Rodrik (2003)** — closest industrial-policy discovery/imitability rationale.
5. **Detragiache (1998)** — direct early-adopter adaptation-cost/follower externality precedent.

## Surviving classification

Only the joint **decentralized host-government × local adopter × supplier downstream commercialization × follower jurisdiction** feedback remains `POTENTIALLY NOVEL / UNRESOLVED`, and only if Stage 3 makes the supplier/jurisdiction interaction alter an endogenous decision rather than merely add payoffs.
