# Stage 2 Search Log

Search date: 2026-08-28

This log records representative searches and audit routes. It is not a claim that every hit establishes novelty or prior art.

## 1. Strategic experimentation / learning public goods

Representative queries:

- `strategic experimentation free rider information public good Bolton Harris`
- `strategic experimentation exponential bandits Keller Rady Cripps`
- `experimentation innovation success copied timing firms patents`
- `technology adoption adaptation cost early adopters followers externality`

Key findings:

- Bolton & Harris (1999): many-agent experimentation; information public good; free-rider and encouragement effects.
- Keller, Rady & Cripps (2005): inefficiently low experimentation from free riding.
- Acemoglu, Bimpikis & Ozdaglar (2011): firms choose when to experiment; successful experiments can be copied; delayed/staggered equilibrium; IPR affects incentives.
- Detragiache (1998): early adopters pay adaptation cost and generate positive externality for followers.
