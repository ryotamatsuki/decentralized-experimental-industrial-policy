## Closest-paper synthesis

A viable Stage 3 candidate must survive **all three** of the following reductions:

1. Remove supplier/downstream commercial choice → if the result becomes Callander–Harstad, reject it.
2. Remove jurisdictions/public sponsor → if the result becomes Acemoglu–Bimpikis–Ozdaglar or Hausmann–Rodrik, reject it.
3. Remove cross-jurisdiction/local-host structure → if the result becomes ordinary PPI supplier rent/certification/procurement design, reject it.

The surviving mechanism must therefore make these pieces interact, not coexist.
