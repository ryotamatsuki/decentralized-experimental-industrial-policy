# Stage 2 → Stage 3 Contract

## Authorized objective

Generate and rank genuinely distinct strategic mechanisms that answer the frozen research question **after** the Stage 2 novelty kills. Stage 3 may only search for a theorem-generating interaction; it may not revive a killed mechanism by relabeling it.

## Frozen Stage 2 conclusions

The following are not standalone contributions:

- generic experimentation free riding / information-public-good underinvestment;
- self-discovery followed by imitation;
- early-adopter adaptation cost plus follower benefit;
- local information versus national spillover as a generic fiscal-federalism trade-off;
- decentralized versus centralized experimentation as a generic regime comparison;
- intergovernmental experimentation race/duplication without a new margin;
- government as early user/test field/lead customer;
- supplier commercialization, certification, rents, procurement centralization or public-buyer capability by themselves.

The surviving research space is only `POTENTIALLY NOVEL / UNRESOLVED`:

> a decentralized public sponsor/host chooses support or competes for a private technology field trial; a local adopter receives place-specific early-use benefits; the supplier owns downstream commercialization rents generated by the trial; and other jurisdictions benefit from later information/adoption, with the supplier's downstream incentive feeding back into local support, trial location, design or incidence.

## Three mandatory reduction tests

Reject a candidate if:

1. removing supplier/downstream commercialization leaves essentially Callander–Harstad federal experimentation;
2. removing jurisdictions/public sponsorship leaves essentially Acemoglu–Bimpikis–Ozdaglar or Hausmann–Rodrik appropriability/self-discovery;
3. removing cross-jurisdiction host/follower structure leaves ordinary public procurement of innovation, certification or supplier-rent design.

## Stage 3 may

- generate 8–12 candidate strategic loops;
- introduce the minimum primitive required for each loop;
- perform targeted mini-searches for new literature families;
- use reduced-form/sign reasoning only to test internal coherence;
- rank candidates with pre-specified weights;
- select at most TOP 3 and one preferred candidate if defensible;
- write a Stage 4 model skeleton and 2–5 conjectures for human review.

## Stage 3 may not

- write formal payoff/utility/demand functions;
- solve an equilibrium or derive FOCs/SOCs;
- prove a theorem;
- add several mechanisms simultaneously;
- claim novelty from search failure;
- move automatically to Stage 4.

## Mandatory stopping rule

Even if Stage 3 returns `GO / GO TO MINIMAL MODEL`, the run **must stop after Stage 3**. Human approval is required before any Stage 4 work.
