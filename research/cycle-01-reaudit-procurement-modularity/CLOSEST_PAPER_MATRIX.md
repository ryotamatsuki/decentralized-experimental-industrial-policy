## Reading of the matrix

The nearest literature is not one isolated paper. It is a tight triangle:

\\[
\text{dynamic procurement/future competition}
+
\text{strategic compatibility/openness}
+
\text{modularity/vertical organization}.
\\]

The candidate must therefore produce a non-additive interaction among these elements. A model that merely places a public buyer before a standard openness game is structurally contained.
