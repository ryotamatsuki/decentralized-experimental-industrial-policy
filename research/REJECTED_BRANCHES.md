# Rejected Branches

Negative results and killed framings remain part of the project record.

## Stage 0 rejections

### Triangle Ehime case description as the contribution

- **Decision:** `REJECTED`
- **Reason:** institutional specificity is motivation, not model/proposition-level novelty.
- **May not be rescued by:** adding descriptive detail or renaming the case as a theory.

### Normative presumption that local industrial policy is desirable

- **Decision:** `REJECTED`
- **Reason:** the project must permit laissez-faire, centralization, or no intervention to dominate under identifiable conditions.

### First mover plus informational free riding as a standalone novelty claim

- **Decision:** `REJECTED AS A PRIOR NOVELTY CLAIM`
- **Reason:** it risks being a direct restatement of experimentation and externality logic. Stage 2 must test it against federal-experimentation prior art.

### Omnibus model containing all eight proposed frictions

- **Decision:** `REJECTED`
- **Reason:** it would obscure the strategic loop and violate the minimal-mechanism discipline.

### Supplier as a label-only extra player

- **Decision:** `REJECTED`
- **Reason:** a supplier matters only if commercialization, evidence appropriation, effort, pricing, or diffusion feeds back into government/adopter choices.

## Stage 1 rejections / bounded claims

### Triangle Ehime as evidence of strategic cross-prefecture free riding

- **Decision:** `REJECTED AS AN INSTITUTIONAL FACT`
- **Reason:** official documents verify evidence production, outward expansion and diffusion support, but not another jurisdiction strategically waiting to avoid trial cost.

### Persistent local first-mover rent as a verified primitive

- **Decision:** `NOT VERIFIED`
- **Reason:** settlement, ecosystem formation and branch-location are program objectives; they do not establish persistent causal rents.

### Inherent prefectural information superiority

- **Decision:** `NOT VERIFIED`
- **Reason:** local-needs and implementation-site search are documented, but superiority relative to national information is a fiscal-federalism hypothesis.

### Government absorbs all trial failure risk

- **Decision:** `REJECTED`
- **Reason:** material public implementation support is verified, but private effort, opportunity cost and residual downside are not eliminated by the program documents.

### All project technology/IP becomes public property

- **Decision:** `REJECTED AS UNSUPPORTED`
- **Reason:** IP/data governance and reporting are required, but management-deliverable copyright language does not establish universal project-level IP ownership.
