# Stage 5 Skeptical Referee Attack — C4 with q_i

## Referee mandate

Attempt to kill the hardened C4 after granting the mathematics. Do not reward the model merely because the Stage 4 blocker is no longer algebraically separable.

## Attack 1 — Is this just competition for a high-quality location?

Partly. The high-q jurisdiction has a higher allocation value because its hosting produces more common evidence. But the more precise representation is even less favorable to novelty: the loser's payoff depends on **which jurisdiction wins**. That is the standard identity-dependent-externality auction structure.

**Result: FAIL for novelty.**

## Attack 2 — Is q_i just a location-quality parameter?

It is more disciplined than a generic quality parameter because the `q_i` effect disappears when `E=0`; it enters only through information produced by the experiment. This passes the microfoundation test.

However, being a legitimate primitive does not make its strategic effect new.

**Result: PASS on interpretation; FAIL as novelty rescue.**

## Attack 3 — Is mislocation generated mechanically by q_1 != q_2?

No unique low-quality mislocation is generated. The active bidding block selects the high-q site. A low-q host appears only in the volunteer/co-ordination block through equilibrium multiplicity and with positive probability in the completely mixed equilibrium.

This is a useful negative finding: the one permitted heterogeneity does **not** mechanically manufacture the desired mislocation proposition.

**Result: conjecture downgraded.**

## Attack 4 — Is the local/social information wedge just a standard externality?

Yes. Moving from q_2 to q_1 raises each jurisdiction's information value by `E Delta_q`, while the planner counts two jurisdictions and obtains `2E Delta_q`. The under-experimentation width is `q_1E/k`.

The exact coefficients are clean, but the welfare wedge is standard partial internalization.

**Result: FAIL as independent contribution.**

## Attack 5 — Has the Slattery/Mast threat been cleared?

Only partially. q_i disciplines the rival bid, which is absent from the Stage 4 symmetric model. But the low-reserve game remains a subsidy/location auction whose valuations differ across governments; the classic fiscal-rent logic remains.

**Result: NOT CLEARED.**

## Attack 6 — Does Callander–Harstad absorb the experiment-selection idea?

It does not contain the same private supplier hosting auction. But heterogeneous districts, informational spillovers, and decentralized distortion of experimentation are already central there. Hence the information side cannot carry C4 alone.

**Result: strong component overlap.**

## Attack 7 — Does public-procurement/lead-market theory absorb the supplier side?

The Stage 5 model keeps supplier downstream value V as an exogenous continuation value and adds no new supplier strategic action. Consequently the supplier is mostly the owner/allocator of the scarce project with a reserve condition.

**Result: supplier does not create an additional new loop.**

## Attack 8 — Is q_i decorative?

No. It changes the losing payoff, contest values and equilibrium subsidies. It is not decorative.

But its nondecorative role is exactly what moves the model into the known auction-with-externalities class.

**Result: blocker solved but novelty still fails.**

## Attack 9 — Is the surviving result merely comparative advantage/location choice?

The information-sensitive bid is richer than simple comparative advantage because the quality of the rival host changes the outside option. Still, the corresponding strategic form is well known in allocation problems with externalities.

**Result: insufficient distance from known theory.**

## Attack 10 — Does this yield a new condition for when local governments should host/subsidize technology experiments?

It yields useful policy diagnostics:

- information quality can reduce wasteful host bidding;
- if `E Delta_q >= H`, the lower-q region will not pay to steal a privately viable trial from the high-q site;
- a high-q site can require central/higher-level financing when local benefits do not cover the financing gap;
- coordination can permit a lower-q volunteer even when the high-q site is socially better.

But these are applications/threshold specializations of known externality-auction and public-good structures, not yet a new economic theory result.

**Result: policy relevance survives; main-theory novelty does not.**

## Fatal governance question

Can a second permissible adjustment create a novel interaction?

Possibly—but every obvious route (host-dependent supplier commercialization, endogenous evidence effort, two knowledge stocks, private local information, dynamic diffusion, disclosure, multiple suppliers, H_i/C_i/V_i heterogeneity) is a **second substantive modification** and is explicitly prohibited at Stage 5.

The correct response is therefore not another repair.

## Referee verdict

**`NO-GO` for C4 as the main theory branch.**

The Stage 5 modification is economically coherent and mathematically successful, but the hardened model is structurally contained by known auction-with-externalities / local-subsidy / volunteer-public-good / experimental-site literatures. Under the project governance, C4 must terminate rather than accumulate a second mechanism.
