# Product-Design Frontier — A1 Novelty Kill

Date: 2026-08-28
Agent C role: Product-Design Killer

## Question

If scalability is a supplier choice rather than an exogenous type, does S/F/R generate a distinct theory of reusable design, or does it reduce to standard endogenous quality/commonality/customization with a larger future market?

## Closest formal and analytical literature

### Jonnalagedda & Saranga (2017), European Journal of Operational Research

**Evidence maturity:** `PUBLISHED MODEL / FULL PUBLISHER PREVIEW VERIFIED`.

The manufacturer chooses product commonality for geographically separate markets. The model explicitly compares primary-market-centric design with design-for-multiple-markets and derives optimal commonality from scale-economy versus preference-mismatch trade-offs.

**Threat:** A1's bespoke-versus-reusable margin is a direct analogue of customization-versus-commonality across markets/sites.

### Jost (2024), Marketing Letters

**Evidence maturity:** `PUBLISHED FULL-TEXT HTML VERIFIED`.

Firms choose the scope of customization to expand into new customer segments. Market expansion and competition determine whether/no/partial/full customization is privately optimal.

**Threat:** a downstream replication market changing an earlier design choice is standard market-expansion/product-design logic. R need not be a special policy mechanism to affect design.

### Brusoni (2023), Industrial and Corporate Change

**Evidence maturity:** `PUBLISHED MODEL/ABSTRACT VERIFIED`.

Firms choose modular products supporting multiple standards versus integral products supporting one standard. Modularity broadens portfolios and changes the optimal timing/strategy of market entry.

**Threat:** reusable/modular design undertaken to serve multiple environments/standards is occupied independently of public policy.

### Hsu, Lu & Ng (2014), Journal of Economic Behavior & Organization

**Evidence maturity:** `PUBLISHED THEORY / PUBLISHER INTRODUCTION VERIFIED`.

Firms allocate production between customized and standardized products; larger market size can strengthen the incentive to customize through competition.

**Threat:** market size affecting the degree of customization/standardization is a standard strategic-design comparative static. A1 cannot claim R->design as novel.

### Constantatos & Perrakis (1998); Zhang (2019); McCannon (2019); Zhang, Zhong & Gao (2025)

**Evidence maturity:** `PUBLISHED MODEL/ABSTRACT VERIFIED`.

Minimum-quality-standard models connect public standards to free entry, quality timing, entrepreneurial innovation and product R&D.

**Threat:** S->endogenous design is occupied, including welfare-reversing effects and entry/design interactions.

### Toshimitsu (2003), International Journal of Industrial Organization

**Evidence maturity:** `PUBLISHED MODEL/ABSTRACT VERIFIED`.

R&D subsidies change endogenous product quality.

**Threat:** F->design is standard innovation-policy/product-quality theory.

## Reduction of the three A1 arrows

1. **S -> design:** public minimum/performance/eligibility standards can change endogenous quality or innovation.
2. **F -> design:** R&D/innovation support can change endogenous quality/design investment.
3. **R -> design:** market expansion/multi-market opportunity changes commonality, modularity or customization.

Each arrow exists in mature theory without Triangle Ehime.

## Does combining the arrows create a new object?

The candidate triple would survive only if the architecture creates a behavior unavailable in all pairwise models—for example, a design choice that exists only because S changes the marginal response to F *and* R in a way not reducible to ordinary threshold/market-size comparative statics.

The audit finds no such primitive or behavioral margin in the frozen A1 definition. With reusable design as the only endogenous design variable, S is a constraint/standard, F lowers its cost, and R raises the market return to the same design. That is a non-separable optimization problem in an algebraic sense, but not a distinct economic mechanism.

## A1-P3 test

> A downstream replication opportunity can increase scalable design incentives only when early validation permits reuse across adopters.

This is strongly threatened by multi-market commonality/modularity and market-expansion/customization theory. Reuse/commonality is precisely what makes a wider market valuable to an earlier design investment.

**Classification:** `STRUCTURALLY CONTAINED`.

## Government substitution test

Replace the public programme with a private accelerator, platform, systems integrator or large corporate buyer that:

- requires multi-client applicability;
- co-finances adaptation;
- exposes successful projects to a larger downstream customer base.

The design incentives remain. Publicness is not needed for the central product-design effect. This does not alone kill a general IO contribution, but the general IO mechanism is already mature.

## Product-design-killer conclusion

`A1 ENDOGENOUS-DESIGN INTERPRETATION = STRUCTURALLY CONTAINED AT THE MECHANISM-FAMILY LEVEL.`

No exact paper reproducing Triangle Ehime's administrative S/F/R package was found. The kill instead follows from structural reduction: the three economically operative arrows map to known standard/finance/market-scope effects, and the frozen architecture supplies no irreducible extra strategic response.
