# Procurement Frontier — A1 Novelty Kill

Date: 2026-08-28
Agent D role: Procurement Killer

## Reduction tested

Can A1 be represented as:

`qualification / performance requirement + implementation cost support + follow-on demand opportunity`?

## Closest procurement and conditional-support literature

### Drake & Xu (2025)

A procurement model with both eligibility regulations and contractual requirements, endogenous bidder entry and bidding. This is direct prior art for public requirements changing participation/composition.

**Classification:** `STRUCTURALLY VERY CLOSE` to S and the participation burden that F offsets.

### Davies & Ellis (2007)

Tax incentives used to attract FDI are explicitly paired with performance requirements. The paper formalizes policy conditionality and transfers in a multi-stage location setting.

**Classification:** `STRUCTURALLY VERY CLOSE` to the generic `condition + subsidy` architecture, although its substantive externality/location mechanism is not imported into A1.

### Chiappinelli, Giuffrida & Spagnolo (2025), IJIO survey

The current PPI survey organizes the economics around innovation effects, procurement design, frictions and PPI's role in the innovation policy mix. It confirms that demand-pull, procurement design and complementarity with other instruments are already a mature research area.

**Classification:** `STRUCTURALLY CLOSE / FRONTIER SURVEY`.

### Dai, Li & Chen (2021)

Public procurement is modeled/analyzed as direct demand pull plus an indirect certification channel. The demand-pull channel is explicitly described as well acknowledged.

**Classification:** `STRUCTURALLY CLOSE` to R as downstream public demand, but A1 is not allowed to use certification as a rescue mechanism.

### Brown & Hendry (2009)

Government-funded demonstration projects and field trials connect real-use testing to later commercialization/diffusion; demonstrations can test technologies in different operational environments.

**Classification:** `COMPONENT OVERLAP / PILOT-TO-COMMERCIALIZATION`.

### Beise (2004)

Lead-market theory explains why innovations developed/adopted in an initial region may diffuse more widely and how firms can use such markets in product development.

**Classification:** `STRUCTURALLY CLOSE` to a regional reference-market story.

### Cao, Yang, Guo & Appolloni (2025)

An evolutionary-game analysis explicitly combines government procurement and R&D subsidy and studies the balance of the two instruments.

**Classification:** `STRUCTURALLY CLOSE` to F+R generic push/pull support.

## Exact-prior-art search result

No single verified paper in this audit was found with Triangle Ehime's exact administrative sequence of:

`multi-site eligibility -> public implementation finance -> separate downstream replication support`.

That absence is **not** sufficient for novelty. The relevant question is structural containment.

## Structural reduction

- qualification/eligibility changing entry is standard procurement economics;
- fiscal support conditional on requirements is standard policy conditionality;
- downstream procurement/demand is standard demand-pull/lead-market logic;
- procurement + R&D subsidy is already treated as a policy mix.

With no verified ex-ante follow-on contract/commitment and no additional primitive, A1's R block is not strategically different from a larger expected downstream market.

## Procurement-killer verdict

`NO EXACT ONE-PAPER COPY, BUT PROCUREMENT/POLICY-CONDITIONALITY THEORY CONTAINS THE OPERATIVE COMPONENTS.`

This reduction becomes fatal when combined with the decomposition and sequencing tests: the triple supplies no behavior that cannot be generated by known pairwise relations.
