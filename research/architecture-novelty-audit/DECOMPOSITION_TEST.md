# Decomposition and Non-Additivity Test

Date: 2026-08-28

## Standard

A1 can advance only if S+F+R generates an endogenous behavior that does not arise from S, F, R or any pair and that is not simply a familiar comparative-static interaction among a standard/constraint, a cost subsidy and market size.

## S only — scalability / multi-site eligibility

Likely effects:

- raises the cost or feasibility threshold for entry;
- selects already-scalable projects under exogenous-type interpretation;
- can induce a higher/broader quality/design under endogenous-design interpretation;
- can reduce total entry.

**Prior-art status:** occupied by entry regulations, minimum quality/performance standards, and standard-induced innovation.

## F only — implementation finance

Likely effects:

- expands participation in implementation;
- changes investment/design when implementation/R&D costs are subsidized;
- can change project quality/composition if projects have heterogeneous private returns/costs.

**Prior-art status:** occupied by innovation subsidy/R&D-finance/endogenous-quality literature.

## R only — downstream replication opportunity

Likely effects:

- raises expected market size for a reusable product;
- changes product commonality/modularity/customization;
- increases incentive to enter markets or develop general-purpose offerings.

**Prior-art status:** occupied by market-expansion, multi-market product-design, modularity, demand-pull and lead-market literature.

## S + F

Interpretation:

`costly qualification/performance condition + fiscal support`.

The two instruments can have opposing entry effects: S restricts; F relaxes participation. This can generate thresholds and changes in entrant composition.

**Prior-art status:** standard eligibility/requirements + participation finance; also close to performance-requirement + fiscal-incentive theory.

**Novel three-way behavior?** No. The entry-restricting versus entry-expanding interaction already exists without R.

## S + R

Interpretation:

`minimum/broad-fit standard + larger future market`.

S pushes design toward compliance/broad applicability while R raises the private return to a design that serves more downstream users.

**Prior-art status:** minimum-standard/endogenous-quality plus multi-market commonality/modularity/market-expansion theory.

**Novel three-way behavior?** No. Design response exists without F.

## F + R

Interpretation:

`supply-side implementation/R&D subsidy + downstream demand-pull support`.

**Prior-art status:** innovation policy mix, public procurement + R&D subsidy, demand-pull/supply-push policy interactions.

**Novel three-way behavior?** No. The push/pull interaction exists without S.

## S + F + R

The strongest A1 advocate formulation is:

- S makes reusable/multi-site implementation costly and can reduce entry;
- F finances part of that cost and therefore changes who accepts S;
- R increases the later market return to reusable design;
- the project pool consequently shifts.

This is genuinely **non-separable algebraically**: the marginal value of a subsidy can differ when a binding standard exists, and a market-size increase can change the attractiveness of paying the compliance/design cost.

But the novelty gate requires **economic non-additivity**, not mere cross-partials/threshold interaction. Under the frozen primitives the triple adds no new strategic margin beyond:

1. comply/design versus not;
2. enter versus not;
3. receive a cost transfer;
4. earn value from a larger market.

All four margins are standard, and every pair needed to generate the qualitative A1 effects is already occupied.

## Can S change the marginal effectiveness of F or R?

Yes, mechanically. If S imposes a costly design/implementation threshold, F is more valuable to projects near that threshold; likewise R raises the return to crossing it. But minimum-standard/entry and endogenous-quality models already generate such threshold-dependent policy effects. This does not establish a distinct architecture mechanism.

## Project-composition test

Candidate project dimensions—scalable/bespoke, broad/narrow applicability, modular/customized, high/low multi-site feasibility—can all be represented as either:

- an exogenous type screened by S; or
- an endogenous quality/commonality/design choice.

Both representations are occupied by prior theory. Introducing further dimensions merely to manufacture a novel composition result would violate minimality and the no-rescue rule.

## Three-way-only behavior test

**Question:** What behavior first appears at S+F+R and cannot occur in S+F, S+R or F+R?

**Answer:** None identified within the frozen primitives.

The triple changes parameter regions and magnitudes, but not the set of strategic margins.

## Verdict

`THREE-WAY ARCHITECTURE CLAIM = FATAL DECOMPOSITION FAILURE.`

A1 fails the required non-additivity condition. The failure is structural and does not depend on finding an exact Triangle-Ehime-lookalike paper.
