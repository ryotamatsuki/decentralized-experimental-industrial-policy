# Skeptical Referee Tournament — A1 Scalability-by-Design

Date: 2026-08-28
Agent H: architecture advocate
Agent I: skeptical referee
Agent J: reproducibility audit reflected in `SEARCH_LOG.md`

The advocate was restricted to the four allowed substantive primitives: scalability/reusability, multi-site implementation cost, public implementation support, downstream replication opportunity. No rescue primitive was added.

## Attack 1 — “This is standard screening.”

**Finding:** If scalability is an exogenous project characteristic, S simply changes which projects enter/pass. Public procurement entry regulations and MQS/free-entry models already generate this composition effect.

**Verdict:** `FATAL`.

## Attack 2 — “Implementation finance is just a participation subsidy.”

**Finding:** Under the frozen architecture F lowers implementation/compliance cost. Any richer screening-finance result would require information/contracting primitives not authorized here.

**Verdict:** `FATAL` as an independent A1 mechanism.

## Attack 3 — “Replication support is just larger market size.”

**Finding:** Primary sources verify downstream opportunity/support but not a binding universal future commitment. Market-expansion, commonality/modularity, lead-market and demand-pull theory already make market scope affect design/innovation.

**Verdict:** `FATAL`.

## Attack 4 — “Multi-site eligibility merely selects an exogenous scalable type.”

**Finding:** Correct under Interpretation 1.

**Verdict:** `FATAL` for selection-only A1.

## Attack 5 — “Endogenous scalable design is standard product design.”

**Finding:** MQS can alter innovation/product R&D; multi-market/commonality/modularity models make broad market scope affect design.

**Verdict:** `FATAL` for the endogenous-design escape.

## Attack 6 — “Three known ingredients do not create new economics.”

**Finding:** S+F, S+R and F+R each reproduce the relevant selection/participation/design responses. The triple changes thresholds/magnitudes but introduces no new strategic margin.

**Verdict:** `FATAL`.

## Attack 7 — “Sequencing is irrelevant.”

**Finding:** With S/F/R anticipated before entry/design, administrative order does not change the choice problem. Making order matter requires learning, sunk investment, hidden information or commitment—new mechanisms.

**Verdict:** `FATAL`.

## Attack 8 — “Government is not essential.”

**Finding:** A private accelerator/platform/corporate buyer can require multi-client fit, co-finance implementation and offer access to downstream customers.

**Verdict:** `WEAKENED` rather than independently fatal; a novel general-IO result could survive public non-essentiality, but that result is not found here.

## Attack 9 — “Welfare follows mechanically from assumed value of scalable projects.”

**Finding:** Without an additional social wedge, making scalable projects socially preferable simply by assumption would make welfare conclusions tautological. Adding a new public-good/externality wedge is forbidden.

**Verdict:** `FATAL` to a welfare-based novelty claim.

## Attack 10 — “Triangle Ehime only provides unusual institutional packaging.”

**Finding:** The package is empirically distinctive and useful motivation, but novelty requires a general economic mechanism. Once translated into screening, cost support and market scope, the economics remains standard.

**Verdict:** `FATAL`.

# Advocate's strongest allowed formulation

The strongest version without feature accumulation is:

> A costly scalability standard may reduce entry; implementation support may offset that cost; a future replication market raises the return to reusable design. The policy package can therefore shift the funded portfolio toward scalable projects even while one instrument restricts and another expands entry.

This is coherent, tractable and policy-relevant. It does **not** pass the novelty gate because every operative margin and each required pairwise interaction is already available in nearby theory.

# Most important referee question 1

> Why can the economic result not be obtained from a standard screening model in which project type is exogenous and the government simply subsidizes participation?

**Answer:** The audit cannot give a satisfactory reason. Under the selection interpretation, that model obtains the core result directly. Endogenizing project design only moves the object into standard-regulation/product-design theory.

# Most important referee question 2

**Answer:** Again, the audit cannot give a satisfactory reason. R's verified role is an expected downstream market opportunity; multi-market design/commonality/modularity theory already maps market scope into design.
