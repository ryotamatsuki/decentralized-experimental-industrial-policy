# Screening Frontier — A1 Novelty Kill

Date: 2026-08-28
Agent B role: Screening/Theory Killer

## Question

Can A1 be reduced to a standard eligibility/screening rule that changes entry/project composition, with F relaxing participation and R adding downstream value?

## Closest theory

### Drake & Xu (2025), Journal of Public Economic Theory

**Evidence maturity:** `PUBLISHED MODEL/PROPOSITION VERIFIED / FULL-TEXT HTML VERIFIED`.

The procuring entity announces entry regulations and contractual requirements before potential bidders decide whether to enter. Eligibility criteria impose entry costs and contractual requirements alter expected payoffs; both affect endogenous bidder entry and bids. This is direct formal prior art for an imposed public requirement changing the participating pool. It does not contain A1's downstream replication stage or endogenous scalable product design.

**A1 implication:** S changing entry/composition is not new economics. If F merely offsets the cost of satisfying S, the S+F core is a participation/entry-cost problem.

### Constantatos & Perrakis (1998), Journal of Regulatory Economics

**Evidence maturity:** `PUBLISHED ABSTRACT/MODEL VERIFIED`.

A minimum quality standard (MQS) operates in a free-entry vertically differentiated market. Crucially, welfare effects depend on whether quality is chosen before or after the entry decision. An MQS can improve welfare in one timing but be redundant or induce monopoly in another.

**A1 implication:** the distinction between **selection** and **inducement**, and the economic relevance of the order of entry/design relative to a standard, already have a formal home.

### Zhang (2019), Mathematics and Computers in Simulation

**Evidence maturity:** `PUBLISHED MODEL/ABSTRACT VERIFIED`.

A multi-stage game studies market entry under an MQS. Intermediate standards generate heterogeneous entry configurations and distinct qualities; high standards can block entry.

**A1 implication:** a scalability/quality threshold that reduces total entry while changing the quality composition of entrants is structurally very close to A1-P1.

### McCannon (2019), Journal of Entrepreneurship and Public Policy

**Evidence maturity:** `PUBLISHED MODEL/ABSTRACT VERIFIED`.

A game-theoretic vertical-differentiation model makes entrepreneurial innovation endogenous under an MQS. The standard can reduce entrepreneurial effort and even lower average expected quality.

**A1 implication:** replacing exogenous scalable types with endogenous design/innovation does not by itself escape the standard-regulation literature.

### Zhang, Zhong & Gao (2025), Journal of the Operational Research Society

**Evidence maturity:** `PUBLISHED MODEL/ABSTRACT VERIFIED`.

Government-set MQS changes product R&D and quality choices under Cournot/Bertrand competition.

**A1 implication:** 2025 frontier evidence directly links a public minimum standard to endogenous product R&D. The proposition that S can induce design effort is occupied at the mechanism-family level.

### Toshimitsu (2003), International Journal of Industrial Organization

**Evidence maturity:** `PUBLISHED MODEL/ABSTRACT VERIFIED`.

Product quality is chosen endogenously before production and R&D subsidies alter quality/welfare.

**A1 implication:** F affecting endogenous product design/quality is not a new primitive.

### Takalo & Tanayama (2010), Journal of Technology Transfer

**Evidence maturity:** `PUBLISHED ABSTRACT VERIFIED`.

Innovation subsidy allocation is studied under adverse selection and project financing frictions. This literature confirms that government selection/screening and subsidy design jointly determine supported projects.

**A1 implication:** it is strong containment evidence for screening+finance, but private information is not an authorized A1 primitive and cannot be used as a rescue mechanism.

### Lach, Neeman & Schankerman (2021), AEJ: Microeconomics

**Evidence maturity:** `PUBLISHED MODEL/ABSTRACT VERIFIED`.

Government R&D finance is explicitly treated as a mechanism-design problem with project heterogeneity and financing terms.

**A1 implication:** sophisticated screening-finance interactions are mature; A1 cannot claim novelty from government financing being conditional on project characteristics.

### Carnehl, Ottaviani & Preusser (2025; NBER WP 2024), Designing Scientific Grants

**Evidence maturity:** `PUBLISHED OVERVIEW / PROPOSITION-FAMILY VERIFIED`.

Grant selection rules affect researchers' incentives to apply and invest in proposals; staged grant design is explicitly discussed. The authors also identify the interaction of grant stages/instruments as an area needing further work.

**A1 implication:** staged selection-induced investment is not a blank slate. The stated open area is not evidence that A1's specific triple is novel.

## Type-screening reduction

Under the exogenous-type interpretation:

- S imposes a rule that only scalable/high-fit projects can pass or makes entry more costly for low-fit projects;
- F changes the participation margin by lowering implementation cost;
- R raises the value of a project that can reach downstream users.

No new strategic variable is required to obtain the composition effect. Existing entry/quality-standard and public-finance theory already generates selection, participation and welfare effects.

**Verdict:** `FATAL` for Interpretation 1.

## Endogenous-design escape test

The attempted escape is that the same supplier chooses bespoke versus reusable design after observing S/F/R. But MQS/product-R&D papers already let a public standard change endogenous quality/innovation, while product-design papers separately let future market scope change commonality/customization. Thus endogenizing design moves A1 into another mature theory family rather than defeating the screening reduction.

**Verdict:** `DOES NOT RESTORE NOVELTY`.

## Candidate propositions threatened

- **A1-P1** — directly threatened by MQS/free-entry work.
- **A1-P2** — no exact same wording located, but the finance-induced composition reversal is a combination of standard subsidy/design and standard eligibility effects; it does not establish a new three-way object.
- **A1-P4** — directly threatened by requirements that restrict entry combined with fiscal incentives/participation support.
- **A1-P5** — requires assumed project heterogeneity strong enough to make finance attract bespoke projects; no independent architecture novelty follows.

## Screening-killer conclusion

`A1 TYPE-SCREENING INTERPRETATION = STRUCTURALLY CONTAINED.`

The only remaining candidate distinction would have to come from an irreducible three-way interaction with endogenous design. The product-design audit tests that escape separately.
