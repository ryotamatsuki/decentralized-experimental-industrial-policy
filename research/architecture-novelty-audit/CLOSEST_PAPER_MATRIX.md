## Matrix reading

### What is not found

No verified source combines all of the following in one formal model: a multi-site/scalability eligibility rule, public implementation finance, a separate downstream replication opportunity, endogenous entry/design, and the exact Triangle Ehime sequence.

### What is found

The economically operative margins are heavily occupied:

- **public requirement -> endogenous entry/composition:** Drake & Xu; MQS/free-entry literature;
- **public standard -> endogenous quality/R&D:** McCannon; Zhang, Zhong & Gao;
- **public finance -> endogenous quality/design:** Toshimitsu and broader R&D-finance theory;
- **future/multi-market scope -> commonality/modularity/customization:** Jost; Jonnalagedda & Saranga; Brusoni; Hsu et al.;
- **push + pull policy interaction:** Guerzoni & Raiteri; Cao et al.; PPI survey;
- **multi-site value as generalization:** Gechter et al.;
- **pilot/field trial -> commercialization:** Brown & Hendry.

## Closest-paper conclusion

There is no `EXACT PRIOR ART` single-paper kill. Nevertheless, the matrix provides a **structural containment kill**: each allowed A1 behavioral margin maps to a mature parent model, and the decomposition test fails to identify a three-way-only strategic response.
