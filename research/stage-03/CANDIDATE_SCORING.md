## TOP 3

1. **C4 — Competition to host a supplier’s scarce launch trial — 89/100**
2. **C6 — Success-biased demonstration design/certification — 89/100**
3. **C3 — Local-fit uncertainty × reusable supplier learning — 85/100**

C4 and C6 tie numerically. C4 is the **preferred candidate** because:

- it most directly answers the project’s normative objective: when a local industrial policy creates an experiment versus merely relocates/transfers rents from an experiment that would occur anyway;
- it uses fewer auxiliary behavioral/political assumptions than C6;
- it naturally separates an **extensive experimentation margin** from a **host/location bidding margin**;
- it can generate both a sharp “do not subsidize” region and an under-experimentation region without assuming that local policy is desirable;
- it is closely connected to the verified institutional pattern of local test fields plus supplier downstream commercialization.

C6 is retained as a high-value alternative because its potential contribution—success rate versus information value—is theoretically sharp, but it faces broader signaling/information-design and political-credit prior art and may require additional primitives.

C3 is retained because it separates public evidence from supplier-owned implementation learning, but a two-knowledge-component structure risks becoming a parameter-driven result unless the interaction is disciplined tightly.

## Fatal-prior-art override

A later discovery of an exact/structurally containing model overrides these scores. High score never authorizes Stage 4 automatically.
