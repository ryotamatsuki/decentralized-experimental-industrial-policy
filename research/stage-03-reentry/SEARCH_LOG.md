## C3 search result

The literature already distinguishes own/private learning from spillover/social learning and directly connects appropriability to industrial-policy/subsidy rationales. A current 2026 preprint further shows that pooled versus fragmented deployment-generated learning interacts with firms' private deployment and product-market incentives. C3's minimum version adds no separate strategic response beyond self-financing/participation.

C3 classification: `STRUCTURALLY VERY CLOSE / NO-GO`.
