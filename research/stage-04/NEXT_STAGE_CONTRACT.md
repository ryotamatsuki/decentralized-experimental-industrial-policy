# Stage 4 → Stage 5 Contract

## Canonical Stage 4 verdict

`CONDITIONAL GO`

## Single diagnosed blocker

The baseline launch-trial information value is invariant to host location. Consequently:

- the hosting bid is determined by `H/(1+mu)` and does not depend on information quality;
- the cross-jurisdiction information benefit `E` affects only whether a government/social planner wants the trial to exist;
- the non-additionality/rent-shifting result remains close to ordinary competition for a mobile project;
- the under-experimentation result remains close to a standard public-good/free-riding threshold.

The experiment-specific and location-competition mechanisms are therefore not sufficiently integrated for a Stage 4 `GO`.

## Exactly one authorized Stage 5 modification

Replace the host-invariant information benefit with **one host-dependent information-productivity primitive**.

Recommended notation:

`q_i > 0`: information/learning quality of a launch trial when jurisdiction `i` hosts.

Stage 5 may make the information/adoption value depend on `q_i` and test whether decentralized host competition selects or subsidizes the trial location differently from the social information-maximizing benchmark.

This is the only substantive change authorized.

## Everything else is frozen

Stage 5 must retain:

- two jurisdictions;
- one supplier;
- one launch trial;
- trial cost `C`;
- supplier downstream commercialization value `V`;
- financing gap `F=C-V`;
- host-only local benefit `H` unless notation requires indexed realization without adding a second new heterogeneity dimension;
- public-finance excess burden `mu`;
- the basic governments-offer / supplier-participates-and-selects-host timing;
- no local private information;
- no political-credit term;
- no endogenous supplier effort;
- no multiple suppliers;
- no endogenous disclosure;
- no branch-location/ecosystem rent;
- no repeated/dynamic experimentation.

## Stage 5 question

Can one host-dependent information-quality primitive make the experiment-producing nature of the project feed back into the host-location competition and generate a nontrivial mislocation / subsidy restriction / governance result that does not collapse to ordinary mobile-firm bidding?

## Stage 5 kill condition

Return `NO-GO` if, after adding `q_i`, either:

1. the location choice is mechanically determined by the assumed `q_i` ranking with no strategic interaction;
2. the local-vs-social wedge is merely an added externality term and the hosting auction remains unchanged;
3. the result is equivalent to standard firm-location subsidy competition with a renamed location-quality parameter;
4. more than this one primitive is needed to make C4 work.

## Prohibition

Stage 5 is not authorized by the Stage 4 run itself. It requires the normal next instruction. Do not add C6, C3, dynamics, private information, matching, disclosure, or additional mechanisms as a rescue.
