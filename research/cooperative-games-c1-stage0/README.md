# C1 Stage 0 — Primitive & Implementability Audit

## Scope

This directory records the Stage-0 audit of **C1 — Nonseparable Coalition-Composition Grants**.

C1 is a new primitive branch from `research/cooperative-games-frontier-scout`; it is not a rescue of A1. A1 remains `A1 NO-GO — ARCHITECTURE ROUTE TERMINATED`. C3/C4/C6/Q3/Q19/Q23 remain closed. C5 and C7 are frozen backups and do not auto-pivot in this run.

## Question

Can a coalition-composition-contingent industrial-policy instrument implement an efficient stable coalition that cannot be replicated by feasible member-level transfers conditional on the same coalition event?

The fatal null is that any monetary coalition subsidy `s(S)` relevant here can be represented by a vector of member payments `t_i(S)` whose sum equals the coalition payment. Stage 0 actively attempts to sustain that null.

## Boundaries

No demand system, Cournot/Bertrand game, parameterized characteristic/partition function, equilibrium, Shapley/core calculation, theorem proof, simulation, SymPy or manuscript is authorized.

## Result

**`NO-GO — C1 TERMINATED AT STAGE 0`**.

The institutional motivation is real: the FY2026 Ehime manufacturing/maritime horizontal-deployment subsidy requires an applicant to establish an implementation structure with a prior Triangle Ehime provider before application. But the grant recipient is the eligible prefectural SME/applicant, not a coalition as such. More fundamentally, once member-level transfers may be contingent on the same observed coalition event, a monetary coalition payment supplies no independent implementability margin under the maintained frictionless-transfer benchmark.

All eight tested rescue primitives fail. Several create a genuinely nonseparable *technology or liability*, but the resulting non-equivalence requires an extra restriction such as unverifiable individual contribution, incomplete contracting, nontransferable utility, direct public provision of an indivisible asset, or joint liability. Those are distinct mechanisms with mature prior literatures; importing them only to defeat `t_i(S)` would change C1 rather than harden it.

No Stage 1 is authorized.
