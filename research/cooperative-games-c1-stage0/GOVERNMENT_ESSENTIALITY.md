# Government Essentiality

Government can be justified as the policy actor when coalition formation generates welfare externalities that private coalition members do not internalize. That establishes a standard public-policy role.

It does not establish C1's instrument distinction.

A lead firm, platform, accelerator, bank, industry association or philanthropic funder could also promise member-specific payments conditional on an observed partnership/composition. Therefore the ability to write `s(S)` instead of `t_i(S)` is not government-specific.

If the state directly supplies an indivisible public input or assumes joint liability, public essentiality can become stronger, but those are different policy primitives and would require a new branch.

**Verdict:** government is not essential to the C1 non-equivalence claim. Public welfare externalities can motivate intervention but do not rescue I4.
