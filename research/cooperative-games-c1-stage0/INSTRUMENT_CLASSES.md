# Instrument Classes

## I0 — Unconditional individual transfer

`t_i`.

No dependence on actions or partners.

## I1 — Own-action-contingent individual transfer

`t_i(a_i)`.

## I2 — Coalition/joint-event-contingent individual transfer

`t_i(S)`.

The public authority can promise member `i` a payment only if the specified coalition/composition/event is realized.

## I3 — Coalition-level but additively decomposable payment

`s(S) = sum_{i in S} t_i(S)`.

This is coalition-labelled accounting, not genuine economic nonseparability.

## I4 — Genuine nonseparable coalition instrument

An instrument or publicly supplied object whose economic/legal claim cannot be decomposed into member-specific claims without changing the feasible action set, liability structure or ownership/usage technology.

The burden of proof is on C1 to establish an I4 object without importing a second mature mechanism.

## I5 — Partition-contingent instrument

`s(S, Pi)`.

This belongs to the frozen C5 backup and is excluded from C1 Stage 0.

## Equivalence benchmark

If the authority observes the same `S`, can condition all payments on the same event, and coalition members can reallocate money frictionlessly, an I3 payment has no implementation advantage over I2. A result that relies only on a different payee label fails C1.
