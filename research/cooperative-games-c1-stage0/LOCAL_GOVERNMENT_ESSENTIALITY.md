# Local-Government Essentiality

The C1 question, if valid, would work essentially unchanged for a national government or other public funder.

Current Ehime evidence is useful because it shows a real subnational programme in which eligibility depends on an implementation relationship with a prior technology provider. But the relevant applicant remains an eligible local business rather than a coalition-owned grant vehicle.

No local-information, intergovernmental competition or specifically subnational primitive is introduced in this audit.

**Verdict:** local government is not essential. The only defensible positioning would have been general industrial-policy theory motivated by a subnational programme; C1 nevertheless fails on instrument non-equivalence before this positioning matters.
