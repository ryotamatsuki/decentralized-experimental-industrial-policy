# Institutional Evidence Audit

## Verified FY2026 Ehime facts

Official Ehime materials for the manufacturing/maritime horizontal-deployment subsidy verify the following.

1. The supported project is an Ehime business introducing a digital implementation result from a prior Triangle Ehime / Triangle Ehime 2.0 selected project.
2. Before applying, the applicant must establish an implementation structure with the prior selected provider.
3. The eligible applicant/recipient is a prefectural SME or other eligible small business entity; the programme does not generally make the supplier–adopter coalition itself the grant recipient.
4. Eligible costs are project-specific costs such as machinery, system construction, expert/outsourcing costs, cloud services, goods and leases.
5. The subsidy is at most one half of eligible costs, capped at JPY 1 million in the manufacturing/maritime scheme.
6. The application is project/applicant based. Payment is generally after project completion and evidence of eligible expenditure. Acquired assets are subject to disposal restrictions attached to the subsidy recipient.
7. The current rules require collaboration/implementation structure with the prior provider, but the reviewed materials do not establish a transferable-utility coalition, a core allocation, joint liability, an indivisible coalition-owned asset, mandatory side payments, coalition-level collateral, or a general binding consortium agreement.

Primary sources checked:
- Ehime Prefecture, FY2026 Triangle Ehime Horizontal Deployment Subsidy (Manufacturing/Maritime) public call page, updated 2026-05-15.
- FY2026 public-call guidelines (`179721.pdf`).
- FY2026 implementing/award rules linked from the same page.
- FY2026 Triangle Ehime 2.0 schedule/guide.

## Evidence boundary

The institutional fact is **relationship-contingent eligibility**: the applicant must have an implementation structure with a designated class of technology providers.

That does not establish **coalition-level monetary nonseparability**. A grant to one applicant whose project requires a partner is economically compatible with an individual transfer conditional on a joint relationship/event (`I2`).

## Implication for C1

The current Ehime case motivates coalition-conditioned eligibility, but it does not supply the primitive needed to distinguish I4 from I2/I3. Treating the implementation relationship itself as proof of a nonseparable coalition grant would overread the source.
