# Stage 1 Institutional Evidence Ledger

Search date: 2026-08-28.

| ID | Proposition | Status | Theory implication |
|---|---|---|---|
| I1 | Ehime solicits private technology providers nationally, identifies local implementation sites, and screens projects. | `PRIMARY-SOURCE VERIFIED` | Public selection and matching are real margins. |
| I2 | Ehime finances implementation trials through project-specific support contracts. | `PRIMARY-SOURCE VERIFIED` | Public support can reduce trial cost. |
| I3 | Trials generate reports on progress, data, outcomes, products/services and IP. | `PRIMARY-SOURCE VERIFIED` | Evidence production is institutionally grounded. |
| I4 | Ehime deliberately supports settlement and horizontal deployment inside the prefecture. | `PRIMARY-SOURCE VERIFIED` | Follow-on adoption is a real policy margin. |
| I5 | Ehime also supports outward expansion of successful projects through national co-creation hubs. | `PRIMARY-SOURCE VERIFIED` | Supplier downstream markets can extend beyond the financing jurisdiction. |
| I6 | FY2026 support is available for a later Ehime adopter to introduce a previously verified success model with the original provider. | `PRIMARY-SOURCE VERIFIED` | Trial to validated model to follower adoption is observed institutionally. |
| I7 | Ehime seeks local retention of outside technology firms and branch/R&D-base establishment. | `INSTITUTIONALLY SUGGESTIVE` | Persistent regional first-mover rent is not established. |
| I8 | Tokyo provides public fields for advance introduction and effectiveness verification, then links success to certification/procurement and sales expansion. | `PRIMARY-SOURCE VERIFIED` | Supplier reputation/commercialization is not Ehime-specific. |
| I9 | NEDO finances and stage-gates private technology demonstrations and links them to diffusion. | `PRIMARY-SOURCE VERIFIED` | National demonstration is a real comparator. |
| I10 | Other jurisdictions strategically wait for Ehime results and avoid equivalent trial cost. | `UNRESOLVED` | Free riding remains a theoretical conjecture. |
| I11 | Host regions retain persistent productivity/ecosystem rents. | `CONJECTURE` | First-mover rent requires a later microfoundation. |
| I12 | Local governments possess information unavailable to the national government. | `CONJECTURE` | Local information use is verified, comparative advantage is not. |
| I13 | Local governments duplicate or race in similar demonstrations. | `UNRESOLVED` | Racing/duplication is not a Stage 1 fact. |
| I14 | All project-level IP belongs to the public sponsor. | `UNRESOLVED` | Management deliverable copyright rules are insufficient. |
