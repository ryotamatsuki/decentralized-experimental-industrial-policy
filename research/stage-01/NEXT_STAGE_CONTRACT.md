# Stage 1 → Stage 2 Contract

## Authorized objective

Determine whether any model/proposition-level research gap survives serious comparison with industrial-policy, federal experimentation, fiscal-federalism, technology-adoption, public-procurement/lead-market, regional/place-based-policy, and political-economy literatures.

## Frozen institutional outputs

Stage 2 must preserve the following:

- public selection/matching of private technology suppliers with local implementation fields is institutionally verified;
- public financing/support of trials is institutionally verified;
- trial outcomes/data are reported and can be deliberately disseminated;
- successful technologies may be horizontally deployed and suppliers may commercialize outside the initial field;
- local retention/branch-location is an explicit policy objective but persistent local rent is not verified;
- national demonstration institutions exist as a real comparator;
- cross-prefecture strategic waiting/free riding is **not** verified;
- local informational superiority over the national government is **not** verified;
- intergovernmental duplication/racing is **not** verified.

## Stage 2 may

- search seminal through 2026 frontier literature and working papers;
- inspect closest papers at model/proposition level wherever reasonably possible;
- classify each contribution claim as exact prior art, structurally very close, component overlap, merely related, or potentially novel;
- kill any Stage 0 mechanism that is already known;
- refine terminology to match the literature;
- perform a targeted mini-search when a candidate distinction opens a new literature family.

## Stage 2 may not

- add a formal payoff, demand, utility, timing or equilibrium model;
- modify institutional facts to manufacture novelty;
- treat “private technology” versus “public policy” as novelty without a distinct strategic interaction;
- treat a supplier as novel merely because prior federal-experimentation papers do not name one;
- infer novelty from search failure;
- rescue a killed claim by adding geography, dynamics, heterogeneity, fixed cost, or an extra player.

## Mandatory kill targets

1. cross-jurisdiction learning/free riding as a standalone contribution;
2. private discovery followed by imitation as a standalone contribution;
3. local-information versus central-spillover trade-off as a standalone contribution;
4. local experimentation racing/duplication as a standalone contribution;
5. public test field/lead customer as a standalone contribution;
6. supplier commercialization as an extra label rather than an endogenous feedback.

## Stage 2 exit rule

Proceed to Stage 3 only if at least one model/proposition-level interaction remains plausibly distinct after closest-paper comparison. Otherwise return `NO-GO` and stop the run.
