# Stage 1 — Source & Institutional Audit

## 1. Executive audit verdict

- **Canonical verdict:** `GO`
- **Routing/status:** `GO TO NOVELTY GATE`
- **Mathematical audit:** `NOT APPLICABLE — no inherited mathematical model exists at Stage 1`

Primary-source audit verifies a generalizable policy object: a public actor selects and supports privately supplied technologies in real implementation fields, finances or reduces trial/implementation costs, monitors evidence, and deliberately supports later diffusion/commercialization. Triangle Ehime is therefore usable as motivation for a broader class of decentralized experimental industrial policy. The audit does **not** verify that other prefectures strategically wait/free-ride, that local first-mover rents persist, or that local governments possess an intrinsic information advantage over the national government.

## 2. Frozen Stage 0 research question

> **When does decentralized selection and financing of private-technology trials generate too little or too much experimentation relative to laissez-faire and national coordination once local project information, cross-jurisdiction learning, and supplier commercialization interact?**

Stage 1 does not modify this question.

## 3. Audited institutional abstraction

A defensible general abstraction is:

1. a public authority identifies local problems/implementation fields and solicits privately supplied technologies;
2. the authority screens or matches suppliers with local adopters/fields;
3. public support lowers or pays implementation/trial costs and may be milestone- or contract-based;
4. projects generate outcome data, reports, implementation know-how, products/services, and potentially IP;
5. the authority evaluates, communicates, or certifies outcomes and may support follow-on adoption;
6. suppliers may receive downstream commercialization/reputation benefits beyond the original field;
7. governance can be decentralized (prefecture/metropolis) or national, creating a legitimate future level-of-government comparison.

This abstraction is institutionally supported without assuming a formal model.

## 4. Triangle Ehime — primary-source findings

### 4.1 Public selection and local matching are real

The FY2025 Triangle Ehime 2.0 specification states that technologies addressing regional problems are solicited nationally, while implementation candidates inside Ehime are actively identified. It requires at least two in-prefecture implementation sites and provides for staged screening of roughly 15 projects. The specification also requires examination of local problems, domestic/foreign cases, technology seeds, spillover measurement, evaluation criteria, and ecosystem formation.

**Status:** `PRIMARY-SOURCE VERIFIED`.

Source: Ehime Prefecture, FY2025 Triangle Ehime 2.0 management/operation specification, pp. 1–3:  
https://www.pref.ehime.jp/uploaded/life/58869_66118_misc.pdf

### 4.2 Public financing/implementation support is material

The FY2025 specification provides for individual implementation contracts and expenditure of implementation-support fees, with project caps and milestone-related payments. The FY2026 management procurement separately budgets a large “digital implementation results horizontal deployment project” and states that individual project implementation support fees are added after project selection.

**Status:** `PRIMARY-SOURCE VERIFIED`.

Sources:  
https://www.pref.ehime.jp/uploaded/life/58869_66118_misc.pdf  
https://www.pref.ehime.jp/uploaded/life/134741_239462_misc.pdf

### 4.3 Experimentation generates recorded evidence

Individual projects are required to report progress, acquired data, generated intellectual property, completed products/services, attained stage, results, and future direction. Study sessions are organized using data acquired through implementation verification.

**Status:** `PRIMARY-SOURCE VERIFIED`.

Source:  
https://www.pref.ehime.jp/uploaded/life/58869_66118_misc.pdf

### 4.4 Diffusion is an explicit policy objective

The FY2025 specification explicitly supports settlement and prefecture-wide horizontal deployment. It also calls for networking with national co-creation hubs for the purpose of expanding high-performing Ehime projects **outside the prefecture**. The FY2026 manufacturing/maritime horizontal-deployment subsidy limits support to introduction of previously verified “Triangle Ehime success models” and requires collaboration with the original adopted project provider.

The FY2026 implementation guideline defines a “Triangle Ehime success model” as an initiative for which implementation verification has been conducted and which is judged effective for introduction by Ehime SMEs.

**Status:** `PRIMARY-SOURCE VERIFIED` for intended/organized diffusion.  
**Not verified:** that another prefecture actually waits strategically and avoids equivalent experimentation costs.

Sources:  
https://www.pref.ehime.jp/uploaded/life/58869_66118_misc.pdf  
https://www.pref.ehime.jp/site/madoguchi/145938.html  
https://www.pref.ehime.jp/uploaded/attachment/179713.pdf

### 4.5 Supplier commercialization and local retention are policy objectives

The FY2025 specification supports continuous business activity by outside-prefecture firms, including introductions to local firms and subsidy consultation, and contemplates branch/R&D-base establishment in Ehime. It also explicitly supports outward expansion of successful projects.

This verifies that the program is concerned with supplier commercialization and local retention. It does **not** prove a persistent regional first-mover rent (`A_R`).

**Status:** `PRIMARY-SOURCE VERIFIED` for program design; `INSTITUTIONALLY SUGGESTIVE` for persistent local benefit.

Source:  
https://www.pref.ehime.jp/uploaded/life/58869_66118_misc.pdf

### 4.6 IP and risk are governed, but exact incidence remains unresolved

The specification requires rules for IP/data management and risk identification, and project reports must include generated IP. The management-procurement document assigns copyrights in management deliverables to Ehime Prefecture, but this does not establish ownership of every technology/project IP right. Public implementation support is verified, but the exact economic incidence of failure risk between government, supplier, and adopter is not fully recoverable from the audited documents.

**Status:** `PRIMARY-SOURCE VERIFIED` for governance requirements; `UNRESOLVED` for project-level IP ownership and complete risk incidence.

## 5. Comparative institutional audit

### 5.1 Tokyo Metropolitan Government — KING SALMON PROJECT

Tokyo explicitly matches startups with government policy problems, provides administrative and other fields for advance introduction, verifies effectiveness, and supports sales-expansion strategy. Successful products/services can receive KING SALMON certification, enabling procurement at Tokyo administrative sites, and the program supports overseas expansion. This directly verifies a public-test-field → evidence/reputation → commercialization pathway outside Ehime.

**Status:** `PRIMARY-SOURCE VERIFIED`.

Sources:  
https://kingsalmon.metro.tokyo.lg.jp/  
https://kingsalmon.metro.tokyo.lg.jp/achievements/

### 5.2 NEDO — national technology demonstration

NEDO’s international demonstration program uses feasibility/design/demonstration/follow-up stages, external review stage gates, and public cost sharing. Its stated purpose is to demonstrate effectiveness under different environments and connect results to domestic and international diffusion.

This verifies a national analogue to publicly supported private-technology demonstration and keeps “local versus national governance” institutionally meaningful.

**Status:** `PRIMARY-SOURCE VERIFIED`.

Source:  
https://www.nedo.go.jp/activities/AT1_00175.html

## 7. What Stage 1 rejects

1. **“Triangle Ehime proves free riding across prefectures.”** Rejected as an institutional fact. The program proves organized diffusion/outward expansion, not strategic waiting by other governments.
2. **“Local first-mover benefits are persistent.”** Not verified. Program retention objectives do not establish a welfare primitive.
3. **“Prefectures necessarily have superior information to the national government.”** Not verified. Local information use is real; comparative informational superiority is a theoretical hypothesis.
4. **“Public funding means government bears all failure risk.”** Not supported.
5. **“Triangle Ehime IP is fully public.”** Not supported by the audited documents.

## 8. Surviving institutional primitives for Stage 2

The literature audit may treat the following as institutionally grounded margins:

- public selection/matching of private suppliers and local adopters;
- technology uncertainty resolved partly through field implementation;
- public financing or cost reduction for trials/implementation;
- evidence production and formal reporting;
- deliberate diffusion/horizontal deployment;
- supplier commercialization/reputation outside the initial field;
- possible local retention/settlement objectives;
- alternative local and national demonstration institutions.

It may **not** treat cross-region free riding, persistent local rents, or local informational superiority as verified facts.

## 9. Mathematical/source verification

There is no inherited mathematical model. Equation-by-equation, FOC/SOC, feasibility, participation, and welfare-identity audits are therefore `NOT APPLICABLE` at Stage 1. No formal utility, payoff, timing, equilibrium or theorem was introduced to fill this gap.

## 10. Verdict and routing

- **Canonical verdict:** `GO`
- **Routing/status:** `GO TO NOVELTY GATE`
- **Blocker:** none requiring Stage 1 continuation.
- **Reason:** the policy object and several strategic margins are independently verified across subnational and national programs, while unverified causal claims remain explicitly bounded.
- **Next stage:** perform model/proposition-level prior-art search; do not alter the Stage 1 abstraction merely to create novelty.
