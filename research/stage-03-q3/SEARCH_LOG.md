## Reproducibility notes

Searches used combinations of mechanism language rather than only innovation-policy labels, including:

- centralized decentralized search market coexistence;
- cream skimming matching market platform;
- referrals search markets;
- two-sided platform investment participation;
- platform search precision governance;
- product design search frictions;
- optimal market segmentation search frictions;
- knowledge hierarchy task allocation matching;
- innovation intermediary demand articulation search.

The search deliberately moved from Q3 vocabulary to parent-theory vocabulary. This is what exposed the fatal reductions.

## Coverage limitation

This is not an exhaustive proof that no distinct Q3 mechanism exists in the literature universe. The Stage-3 decision is instead based on the stronger finding that every tractable candidate generated in this run positively maps to an established formal mechanism class.
