## Why the top scores do not generate a handoff

### M3
The desired feedback is coherent, but its minimal economics is selective routing/cream-skimming across intermediation venues followed by endogenous participation. Bolton–Santos–Scheinkman, Romanyuk–Smolin and centralized/decentralized market-choice theory already occupy that mechanism family.

### M2
Problem formulation genuinely matters institutionally, but the minimal strategic version becomes endogenous product/problem design changing search precision/frictions and supplier participation. Brancaccio–Kang, Zhong, Kanoria–Saban and platform-governance theory make that an unsafe standalone mechanism.

### M10
Escalating difficult cases to a higher-level specialist intermediary is economically coherent but maps directly to knowledge hierarchies/task allocation. Adding a national supplier pool changes the application, not yet the core allocation logic.

## Canonical implication

Numerical ranking cannot override the reduction findings. No candidate satisfies the Stage-3 success criterion of a minimal implementable loop with plausible model/proposition-level distance from the closest literature.
