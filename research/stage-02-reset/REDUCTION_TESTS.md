# Stage 2 Reset — Reduction Tests

## Q3

**Reduction:** “This is fiscal federalism with local information plus a matching platform with central market thickness.”

**Result:** `NOT YET FATAL, BUT BINDING STAGE-3 TEST`.

What could make the reduction false is not the existence of two tasks but an endogenous response: assigning problem articulation/search across levels changes whether and how adopters and suppliers participate/search, changing the thickness/composition of the matching market and thereby feeding back into the value of the assignment.

If that loop cannot be obtained without adding another independent distortion, Q3 must be killed.

## Q19

**Reduction:** “This is learning-by-doing/state-capacity investment by the government or repeated buyer.”

**Result:** `FATAL`.

Current public activity improving future administrative/intermediation capability is already the core logic of endogenous capacity investment or organizational learning. Learning only supplier quality is even closer to repeated procurement. A distinctive supplier/adopter composition response would be a second mechanism not currently contained in Q19.

## Q23

**Reduction:** “This is endogenous bidder entry/costly information acquisition before procurement versus a subsidy after entry.”

**Result:** `FATAL FOR CURRENT STAGE-3 SELECTION`.

Interactive problem formulation may escape the narrow entry-cost reduction, but then the actual mechanism is specification/information co-production. That mechanism is not public-specific and has not been authorized as a replacement Q23 branch.

## Government-removal test

| Candidate | Replace government with private actor | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Q3 | private broker/platform can reproduce much of search design | `WEAKENED`; multilevel public assignment still open |
| Q19 | private repeated buyer/intermediary can learn capability | `FATAL THREAT` |
| Q23 | private sophisticated buyer can conduct supplier engagement | `FATAL THREAT` |

## Ultimate reduction result

Only Q3 retains an unresolved endogenous-margin claim worth testing in Stage 3. This is not a finding that Q3 is novel; it is permission for one mechanism-search attempt under a hard kill contract.
