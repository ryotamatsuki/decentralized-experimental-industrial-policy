## Q3

Strongest formal threats are two-sided platform/search design, platform integration/market thickness, and fiscal-federalism assignment. Kanoria–Saban makes search-environment design endogenous; Zhou et al. makes integration and market thickness welfare-relevant. Edler–Yeow and the innovation-intermediary literature directly occupy demand–supply intermediation institutionally.

Q3 survives only if Stage 3 can show a loop in which allocation of different tasks across intermediary levels changes supplier/adopter participation or search, thereby changing market thickness/match composition and the value of the task allocation. If the model becomes local-information quality versus central scale, or a standard platform search-design problem, kill it.

**Stage-2 status: `POTENTIALLY DISTINCT / UNRESOLVED`.**

## Q19

The minimum version maps to endogenous state-capacity investment, public/organizational learning-by-doing, or repeated procurement learning. Besley–Persson already make current investment determine future state capability; Gur–Macnamara–Saban make repeated procurement generate strategic learning; public-buyer dynamic-capability research documents the procurement capability channel.

The proposed “excessive project activity” side additionally needs a separate divergence from social welfare. Adding political incentives, soft budgets, turnover or another market-composition feedback would be a second mechanism.

**Stage-2 status: `NO-GO`.**

## Q23

The tractable version maps closely to procurement with endogenous entry/pre-contract R&D and costly supplier participation. Tan (1992) and newer endogenous-entry procurement work are direct threats. 2026 market-engagement evidence shows that engagement can involve demand articulation and interactive learning, but that empirical distinction is not yet a distinct public-policy theory mechanism.

The residual co-production channel—supplier interaction changes the problem/specification, which changes the future supplier/solution set—is interesting but is also available to private buyers and would require a new information/design mechanism.

**Stage-2 status: `NO-GO FOR CURRENT STAGE-3 SELECTION`.**
