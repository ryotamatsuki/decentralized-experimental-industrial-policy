Default target: **C1 — Nonseparable Coalition-Composition Grants**.

1. What coalition-specific complementarity makes subset identity economically relevant?

6. What welfare externality prevents private contracting from implementing the social optimum?
7. Why can a private platform/lead firm/VC not implement the same arrangement?
