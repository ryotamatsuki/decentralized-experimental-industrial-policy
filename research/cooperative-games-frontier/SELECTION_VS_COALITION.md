# Selection versus Coalition Formation

## Boundary with killed A1

A1 studied whether scalability eligibility, finance and replication opportunity select or induce better projects. It failed because the economics survived replacement by standard screening/participation/market-size primitives.

The present branch is distinct only when the productive object is not a firm/project type but a **combination of firms whose value and outside options depend on who else participates**.

## Reduction test

If coalition value can be written additively as the sum of individual project qualities and individual participation constraints, coalition selection reduces to individual screening. This is fatal.

A candidate may survive only if at least one of the following is irreducible:

- match/coalition-specific complementarity;
- coalition-specific outside options;
- blocking by subsets;
- rival-coalition externalities;
- exclusivity/multi-coalition membership rights;
- nonseparable policy conditional on composition.

## C6 verdict

`Selecting productive combinations` without a distinct policy/stability result is not enough. Matching, team formation and mechanism design can already select complementary sets. C6 is therefore killed as a standalone branch and its only potentially useful element—nonseparable composition—is folded into C1.

## A1 firewall

No surviving candidate may claim novelty from scalability, implementation finance, or downstream market size. Those primitives can appear only as institutional background, not as the mechanism carrying the new branch.
