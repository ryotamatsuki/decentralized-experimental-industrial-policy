# Primary-Source Institution Map

Date: 2026-08-28

## Scope

This audit reconstructs Triangle Ehime / Triangle Ehime 2.0 from primary public materials before applying economic labels. The central current source is the FY2026 management/operations specification, supplemented by official horizontal-deployment subsidy pages, official programme pages, adoption announcements and budget materials.

## Verified institutional sequence

1. Ehime identifies priority sectors and local implementation needs/fields.
2. The programme recruits technology firms/startups nationally and also recruits firms through external co-creation hubs.
3. Applicants must secure at least two implementation sites in Ehime for the main recruitment route.
4. The programme provides pre-application consultation and matching support between applicants and local implementation candidates.
5. A staged screening process narrows applicants to roughly 20 new projects, with different maximum project-support levels.
6. Selected suppliers receive project-specific implementation contracts; implementation expenditure is inspected and can be paid by milestones.
7. Projects are actively managed, with risk management, progress reporting, problem-resolution support and midterm/final evaluation.
8. Participating firms hold study sessions at least three times per project using data obtained during implementation.
9. The programme provides accompaniment for implementation retention and in-prefecture horizontal deployment.
10. The operator supports applicants in building packaged offerings and promotional materials for use of separate horizontal-deployment subsidies.
11. Separate prefectural subsidies support new adopters in agriculture/fisheries, manufacturing/maritime and disability-welfare fields to introduce solutions previously validated through Triangle Ehime.
12. The architecture also links projects to networking, private finance, university employment matching, AI internships and local branch/R&D-site attraction.
13. The programme follows the retention status of selected projects for roughly five years.

## What is not verified as a universal rule

- The prefecture does not appear to be the final purchaser/user in every Triangle Ehime project.
- Public ownership of all project IP or data is not verified; rather, the specification requires management rules and reporting for data/IP.
- A legally binding ex-ante guarantee of later horizontal-deployment subsidy for every project is not verified.
- Every project does not necessarily receive local-branch or AI-human-capital support.

## Quantitative programme facts used only as institutional context

- FY2026 specification: national recruitment target 500+ applications and about 20 new projects.
- Main recruitment route: two or more Ehime implementation sites required.
- FY2026 official programme announcement: 17 new projects selected; the programme reports about 2,000 applications and 137 selections over FY2022-2026.
- FY2026 continuation selection: 16 continuation projects.
- Programme-level targets include economic effect, local establishment of outside digital firms and digital-human-capital formation.

## Primary-source implication for the research object

The policy cannot be faithfully represented as a scalar subsidy alone. The verified object is a staged public production process that recruits projects, supplies access to real implementation environments, finances and governs implementation, produces evidence/implementation experience, and then supports replication and regional embedding.

The architecture audit therefore treats `publicly orchestrated private technology-market formation` as a candidate research object, not as an established theory contribution.
