## Architecture interpretation

The primary bundle is not merely `search + subsidy`. It contains four linked blocks:

1. **project formation:** local field discovery, national recruitment, pre-matching and multi-site screening;
2. **implementation production:** field access, public cost-sharing, adaptation and project management;
3. **translation to replicable offering:** evaluation, data use, retention support and packaging;
4. **market extension:** adopter-side diffusion support and regional embedding.

The audit therefore asks whether private behavior is different when these blocks are sequenced together, rather than whether each block is useful in isolation.
