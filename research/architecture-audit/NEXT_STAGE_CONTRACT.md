## Selected architecture

**A1 — Scalability-by-Design Architecture**

Frozen candidate question:

> When can a public programme improve the composition of technology projects by combining multi-site scalability requirements, implementation finance and downstream replication support rather than subsidizing pilots or adoption in isolation?

## Frozen minimal bundle

1. **multi-site / scalability eligibility condition**;
2. **public implementation finance/cost sharing**;
3. **downstream replication opportunity/support after validation**.

No fourth policy function may be added in the next audit.

- at least two Ehime implementation sites required for the main FY2026 route;
- publicly supported project-specific implementation contracts;
- implementation management and evaluation;
- retention/horizontal-deployment accompaniment and package-building support;

## Exact fatal reduction

The next audit must try to prove:

> **“A1 is just a standard screening mechanism with an exogenous scalable type, a transfer satisfying participation, and an additive downstream market-size benefit.”**

If accurate, verdict must be `NO-GO`.

## Additional kill tests

Kill A1 if:

- multi-site requirement only reveals an exogenous type;
- implementation finance only relaxes a participation constraint without changing design/composition;
- replication support only adds a separable expected-profit term;
- product scalability is mechanically assumed to increase with public support;
- the same proposition is already contained in a standard lead-market/PPI model;
- government/private-programme identity is purely cosmetic and the general IO result is not itself new;
- a fourth mechanism is required for a nontrivial welfare result;
- the strongest result is merely “three complementary instruments outperform one.”

## What may survive

A1 may proceed beyond the novelty audit only if at least one model/proposition-level distinction remains, such as:

- finance reverses the selection effect of a scalability requirement because scalable design is costly and endogenous;
- downstream replication changes ex-ante design in a way that makes the optimal screening condition depend non-monotonically on public finance;
- an instrument that is beneficial in isolation becomes harmful when it changes the composition of applicants generated by another stage;
- the optimal architecture has distinct no-market / supported-validation / self-sustaining-replication regimes not already contained in the closest theory.

These are search targets, not results.
