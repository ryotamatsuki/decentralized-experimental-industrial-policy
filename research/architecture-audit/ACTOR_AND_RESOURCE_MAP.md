# Actor and Resource Map

Date: 2026-08-28

| Actor | Contributes | Chooses / controls | Receives | What may remain after project |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ehime Prefecture | budget, programme authority, policy priorities, access/coordination capacity | programme design, selection framework, continuation/development support, related subsidy schemes | policy outcomes, regional economic effects, information/evaluation reports | programme knowledge, networks, regional implementation record |
| Programme operator | scouting, programme management, matching, project management, promotion, networking | operational design subject to prefectural agreement; implementation support; stakeholder coordination | management contract | accumulated intermediation/implementation know-how (not assumed publicly owned) |
| Technology supplier / startup | technology, engineers, adaptation work, project proposal | whether to apply; implementation design; effort; local business continuation | implementation contract/support, reference sites, business opportunities, reputation | improved product/service, local customer base, possible local branch/R&D base |
| Local implementation firm / adopter | real operational environment, staff time, data/knowledge, feedback | participation and continued use | adapted technology, productivity/quality benefits, implementation experience | installed solution, organizational capability, relationship with supplier |
| Subsequent local adopter | new demand, local customization effort | whether to adopt validated solution | horizontal-deployment subsidy where eligible, validated solution | private transaction and local diffusion |
| Universities / students | human capital, research/learning interface | participation in internship/employment | training and employment opportunities | local digital talent stock |
| External co-creation hubs | supplier network and discovery access | referral/participation | access to Ehime implementation opportunities | interregional network |
| Local support institutions / finance | financing and network access | financing/participation | deal flow / ecosystem relationships | local innovation network |

## Money

- The programme operator is commissioned by Ehime.
- Selected project proposers receive project-specific implementation contracts/support; milestone-linked payments are explicitly contemplated.
- Separate horizontal-deployment subsidies can support later adopters' customization, functional modification and introduction costs.

## Real resources

- Local implementation sites provide real operating environments, staff time and contextual knowledge.
- Suppliers provide technology and engineering/adaptation capacity.
- The operator provides management, matching, field coordination, consultation and promotion.

## Risk

- The programme absorbs part of the monetary cost of implementation.
- Suppliers retain commercial and execution risk not contractually covered.
- Local adopters bear workflow disruption, staff attention and continued-use risk.
- Failure-risk allocation is project-specific and is not inferred beyond verified rules.

## Rights / data / IP

The specification requires explicit policies for IP and acquired-data management and requires projects to report generated IP and acquired data. Universal public ownership is not verified.

## Follow-on decisions

The architecture does not end at pilot completion. Suppliers may continue local business, construct packaged offerings, seek new adopters, use separate diffusion subsidies, obtain private finance or establish local bases. New local adopters choose whether to transact with validated suppliers.

## Research implication

The potentially scarce object is not only money. It includes real-world implementation access, engineering capacity, adopter managerial attention, reusable implementation design, and the ability to convert a bespoke implementation into a replicable private-market offering.
