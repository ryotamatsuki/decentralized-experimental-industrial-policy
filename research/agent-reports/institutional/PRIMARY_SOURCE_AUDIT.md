# Institutional / Primary-Source Auditor Report

Date: 2026-08-28

## Assignment

Test whether Triangle Ehime is merely a case-specific subsidy description or belongs to a broader policy class with generalizable economic margins. Do not infer strategic behavior from program rhetoric.

## Findings

### Verified in Ehime

- national solicitation of technology providers and active discovery of local implementation sites;
- screening/selection of projects and public implementation-support expenditure;
- project management with milestones, risk management, reporting, acquired-data and IP reporting;
- study sessions using implementation data;
- support for implementation settlement and prefecture-wide horizontal deployment;
- explicit networking intended to expand high-performing projects outside Ehime;
- a 2026 follower-adoption subsidy restricted to previously verified “Triangle Ehime success models” and requiring collaboration with the original provider;
- support for continued local business by outside suppliers and contemplated branch/R&D-base location.

### Verified outside Ehime

Tokyo’s KING SALMON PROJECT provides administrative/private fields for advance introduction, tests effectiveness, and links successful products to certification, public procurement and wider/overseas commercialization. NEDO operates a national staged, publicly cost-shared technology-demonstration program whose stated objective includes proving effectiveness and diffusion.

### Not verified

- strategic waiting by another prefecture for Ehime’s trial result;
- avoided experimentation cost by a specific follower jurisdiction;
- persistent host-region first-mover rent;
- intrinsic local-government information superiority over national government;
- systematic intergovernmental demonstration racing or duplication;
- complete project-level IP ownership and failure-risk incidence.

## Institutional abstraction

The broad policy class is **publicly facilitated private-technology experimentation and diffusion**: a public actor selects/matches private technologies to implementation fields, supports trial costs, observes/evaluates evidence, and can influence follow-on adoption and supplier commercialization. The public actor may be subnational or national.

This abstraction is strong enough for Stage 2. Free riding and first-mover rents remain candidate theoretical primitives rather than documented facts.
