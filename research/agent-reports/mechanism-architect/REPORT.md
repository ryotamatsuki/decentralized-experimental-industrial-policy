# Mechanism Architect — Stage 3 Synthesis

## Assignment

Generate genuinely different mechanisms from the Stage 2 surviving white space without reviving killed contributions, then select a minimum TOP set.

## Output

Eleven candidates were generated. TOP 3 are:

1. C4 — competition to host a supplier’s scarce launch trial;
2. C6 — success-biased demonstration design / certification;
3. C3 — local-fit uncertainty × reusable supplier learning.

## Preferred candidate: C4

C4 is preferred because the private supplier's downstream commercialization value creates a distinct **trial-occurrence** incentive while local governments have a distinct **host-location** incentive. This makes it possible for local public support to be positive even when it has zero additionality for experimentation.

The candidate is not “free riding versus first-mover advantage.” Free riding appears only in the social value of non-host information. The key new candidate wedge is the split between:

- supplier's private incentive to run one trial for downstream commercialization;
- host government's incentive to capture the local early-use/place benefit;
- follower jurisdictions' informational/adoption benefit.

## Why not C6 first

C6 is theoretically interesting but may require a sponsor success payoff, endogenous test difficulty/informativeness, certification/signaling and possibly political incentives. Its literature neighborhood is broader and the minimal model is less obvious.

## Why not C3 first

C3 can be modeled cleanly but risks a result that is mechanically generated by assigning separate appropriability parameters to public evidence and supplier-private know-how.

## Architect recommendation

Send **C4 only** to a human Stage 4 approval gate. Preserve C6 and C3 as rejected-for-now alternatives, not automatic fallback branches.
