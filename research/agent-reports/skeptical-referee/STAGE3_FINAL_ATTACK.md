# Stage 3 Final Skeptical Attack

## Candidate under attack

C4 — Competition to host a supplier’s scarce launch trial.

## Attack 1 — Callander–Harstad relabel

**Risk:** high if cross-region learning is the only wedge.

**Current defense:** C4 requires a private supplier whose downstream commercialization value changes whether the launch experiment is privately viable and who selects among local hosting offers. The proposed headline result concerns support that may alter only the location/rent of a privately viable experiment.

**Stage 4 condition:** if supplier commercialization can be removed without changing the headline result, kill C4.

## Attack 2 — Hausmann–Rodrik / Acemoglu relabel

**Risk:** medium-high if supplier experimentation/copying dominates.

**Defense:** jurisdictions endogenously compete for host status; this location-support game is absent from pure private experimentation.

**Stage 4 condition:** if local host status has no distinct strategic effect, kill C4.

## Attack 3 — ordinary bidding for firms

**Risk:** very high; this is the strongest objection.

**Defense:** the scarce object is an information-producing launch trial, not permanent economic activity. Supplier downstream commercialization can make the experiment occur regardless of subsidy, while the host-only benefit can still induce local bidding. Non-host regions benefit from evidence after the trial.

**Stage 4 condition:** if replacing “trial” with “mobile plant” leaves the equilibrium and welfare results unchanged, kill C4.

## Attack 4 — first-mover rent is assumed

**Risk:** high.

**Defense:** Stage 4 should use the narrowest host-only early-use benefit and should not assume persistent ecosystem rents. The result must not require an arbitrarily large host benefit.

## Attack 5 — free riding mechanically creates underinvestment

**Risk:** fatal if this is the headline theorem.

**Defense:** under-experimentation is only one boundary region. The preferred headline candidate is **non-additional local support / hosting-race waste** when the supplier already wants the trial.

## Attack 6 — supplier adds no strategic interaction

**Risk:** fatal.

**Defense:** supplier downstream rent must move the extensive participation threshold and interact with local bids. Otherwise kill.

## Attack 7 — local information is assumption-driven

**Response:** C4 baseline should omit local private information. It is not needed for the preferred mechanism.

## Attack 8 — central vs local ranking is standard

**Response:** national co-financing is a later policy implication, not the baseline contribution. First prove a new local-support wedge.

## Attack 9 — why local government?

**Response:** because host status creates a geographically localized early-use/place benefit while trial information and supplier commercialization extend beyond the host. If the host-only benefit cannot be defended, a local-government model is unnecessary and C4 should be killed.

## Attack 10 — can the project derive “do not intervene” conditions?

**Yes, conditionally.** If supplier downstream commercialization already makes the launch trial privately viable, local support has no experimentation additionality. If governments nevertheless bid for host status, the model can deliver a policy region in which local intervention should be restricted or avoided. This remains a conjecture until Stage 4.

## Final skeptical verdict

`PASS TO HUMAN HARD GATE, NOT TO AUTOMATIC MODELING`.

C4 is fragile but worth one minimal-model attempt. C6/C3 should not be added to rescue it. No Stage 4 work is authorized by this review.
