# Skeptical Referee — Why This Paper Is Probably Not Novel

## Referee posture

Assume rejection unless a model/proposition-level distinction survives the closest prior art.

## Attack 1 — This is Callander–Harstad with “technology” replacing “policy”

**Substantially correct for the original free-riding pitch.** Jurisdictions already experiment, learn from one another, distort experiments and face centralization alternatives in Callander & Harstad (2015). Any model whose only new object is private technology must be rejected.

## Attack 2 — This is Hausmann–Rodrik / Acemoglu et al. with a prefecture paying the bill

**Correct for discovery/imitation alone.** Private discovery that followers copy and delayed experimentation under weak appropriability are already standard.

## Attack 3 — This is ordinary place-based subsidy competition

**Correct if governments simply bid for local firms/jobs.** Local fiscal competition and bidding-for-firms are mature literatures. A host-region benefit parameter plus subsidy bidding is insufficient.

## Attack 4 — First-mover advantage is inserted by assumption

**Current concern is valid.** Stage 1 did not verify persistent local rent. A Stage 4 model must either derive the host-only benefit from early use/location or clearly treat it as a minimal primitive and prove the result does not mechanically follow from its size.

## Attack 5 — Free riding mechanically implies underinvestment

**Correct and fatal as a contribution.** This claim is killed.

## Attack 6 — Adding a supplier changes nothing

**Correct for a label-only supplier.** The 2025 IJIO PPI survey and procurement theory already center supplier incentives, commercialization, rents, certification, risk and buyer design. A supplier survives only if its downstream market changes a local government's equilibrium policy or the experiment/location margin.

## Attack 7 — Local information advantage is assumption-driven

**Correct unless separately microfounded.** Local-needs knowledge is institutionally real, but comparative informational superiority is not verified and fiscal-federalism already uses heterogeneity/information wedges. It should not be the baseline headline result.

## Attack 8 — Central versus local welfare ranking is standard

**Correct.** A hybrid-finance result is not enough without a new mechanism.

## Attack 9 — Why must the government be local?

A promising answer exists only if the **host location itself** delivers a local early-use/place benefit or local implementation field that determines trial value/location. If this is absent, use a national procurement model instead.

## Attack 10 — Can the project really explain when policy should not be done?

Potentially yes. The strongest candidate is one in which the supplier's downstream commercialization value already makes the trial occur privately. Then competing local subsidies can change only the host/location or transfer surplus to the supplier rather than increase experimentation. That provides a sharp “do not subsidize” region. The opposite region can still exist when non-host informational benefits are large enough that the trial is socially valuable but supplier+host incentives are insufficient.

## Referee verdict after attacks

- **Broad paper:** reject.
- **First-mover/free-riding paper:** reject.
- **Local-information/federalism paper:** reject.
- **Generic government–supplier demonstration paper:** reject.
- **Narrow host-competition / supplier-downstream-rent interaction:** permit Stage 3 mechanism search only.

The surviving branch must pass three reductions: it cannot collapse to federal experimentation when supplier commercialization is removed, to private copying/self-discovery when jurisdictions are removed, or to ordinary PPI when host/follower geography is removed.
