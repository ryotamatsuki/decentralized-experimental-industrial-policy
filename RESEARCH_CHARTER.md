## Motivation and scope

The motivating institution is the class of subnational programs, exemplified by Triangle Ehime, that help private technology suppliers and local adopters conduct field trials, implementation, and possible diffusion.

> **Triangle Ehime is motivation, not the object of the final theory.**

Program-specific facts are not accepted as model primitives until verified in Stage 1. The intended object is a general theory of decentralized government-sponsored experimentation in private technology adoption.

## Provisional research question

> When does decentralized selection and financing of private-technology trials generate too little or too much experimentation relative to laissez-faire and national coordination once local project information, cross-jurisdiction learning, and supplier commercialization interact?

## Normative objective

Identify conditions under which local intervention is preferable and conditions under which it is not. Candidate comparison regimes are laissez-faire, decentralized/local provision, centralized/national provision, and multilevel or co-financed provision. No ranking is presumed.
